# Documentary edit — 4 November 2026

The narrative spine is a change in participation: an artist releases a work; listeners become recognizable supporters; some enter the creative process; people build systems to keep working together. This is an editorial framing across different experiments, not a claim that all participants shared one philosophy or followed the same path.

## The scrolling journey

1. **Agency before technology.** The opening establishes what artists wanted to change, then uses musicben's explicitly attributed recollection to give that question human stakes. The 2015 Ujo experiment remains available as earlier context rather than being treated as a definitive origin. RAC/Zora's 2020 cassette experiment is explicitly distinguished from NFTs.
2. **A record and a room.** Catalog's founders and Loft Radio precursor lead into the one-of-one record. Songcamp's rooms show how a release could also connect people; Future Surf's recurring programming supplies the ordinary work between launches.
3. **Different ways to participate.** Daniel Allan, Sound editions and Soulection illustrate different arrangements. Optional Eternal Garden and Moonshot chapters examine artist worlds and continuing relationships.
4. **Making together.** CHAOS gives the collective experiment depth, Walls gives it three specific people, and a remix credit provides an evidenced path onward. The Heart Project offers a separate studio ambition, with announcement and outcome carefully distinguished.
5. **Bodies in a room.** LATASHA/Zoratopia connects online activity to gatherings. Black Dave/wavWRLD/Glass/Reveel shows a performance becoming a video and a proposed distribution experiment. Chill Pill opens another question: the person and work behind a virtual identity.
6. **What persisted, what changed.** Platform endings and preservation precede Pete's attributed builder reflection. Coop Records' evolving mechanisms complicate an ending that would otherwise imply everything simply stopped.

The main path remains six movements. Seven optional chapters open from quiet links at chapter breaks; browser Back restores the documentary position. No additional menus interrupt individual paragraphs. The target remains a 6–8 minute reading path; this is not a timed narration or a measured user reading study.
