CAMPFIRE described its first season as songs selected by community vote.

That gave supporters a role before a release reached a collector: helping decide which work the project would put out.

Into 2023 / beyond the gathering

What kept people
coming back?

CAMPFIRE’s credits show what one gathering produced. Keeping a scene going also took less visible work: feedback, teaching and regular conversations. Future Surf’s 2023 schedule gives us a glimpse of that week-to-week life.
